Common questions

Why does QuickClean ask for my home folder?

The App Store build runs in the App Sandbox and can only read folders you grant. Granting your home folder once lets the check-up see caches, downloads and app data. The permission is remembered; you can revoke it in Settings.

Is the check-up free?

Yes. The check-up, overview, large files, duplicates search, space map and history are free. A one-time purchase unlocks processing: moving reviewed items to the Trash, uninstalling apps with their leftovers and duplicate processing. Use Restore Purchases on another Mac signed in with the same Apple ID.

Can I undo a cleanup?

Items are moved to the Trash, not deleted. Open Activity in the sidebar to put them back, as long as the Trash has not been emptied and nothing has been created at the original location.

Why are some findings "detect only"?

QuickClean only processes locations whose rules have passed its safety checks. Everything else is shown with an explanation and a way to manage it in the owning app. Tool commands such as brew cleanup are shown for you to run in Terminal; the App Store build cannot run them.

The numbers differ from Finder or System Settings.

QuickClean reports space actually used on disk. Purgeable space and local snapshots are managed by macOS and shown separately. Sparse files such as virtual machine disks count at their real size, not their maximum size.

Something important was flagged, or a large folder was missed.

Please send feedback with the kind set to match. Reports help us investigate rules and protection lists.

常见问题

为什么要授权个人文件夹？

App Store 版在沙盒中运行，只能读取你授权的文件夹。授权一次个人文件夹，体检才能看到缓存、下载和应用数据。授权会被记住，可以在设置中撤销。

体检免费吗？

免费。体检、概览、大文件、重复文件查找、空间地图和历史都免费。一次性购买解锁处理能力：把审查过的项目移入废纸篓、连同残留卸载应用、处理重复文件。在同一 Apple ID 的其他 Mac 上用「恢复购买」。

清理后能撤销吗？

项目是移入废纸篓而不是删除。在侧栏的「活动」页可以放回原处，前提是废纸篓未清空、原位置没有同名项目。

为什么有些项目只能检测？

只有通过安全检查的规则才允许处理，其余项目会给出说明和在所属应用中管理的方式。brew cleanup 之类的工具命令会显示出来供你在终端运行，App Store 版不能代为执行。

数字和访达或系统设置不一样。

QuickClean 报告的是磁盘实际占用。可清除空间和本地快照由 macOS 管理，单独显示。虚拟机磁盘这类稀疏文件按实际占用计，而不是按最大容量。

重要内容被检出了，或者大文件夹没被发现。
