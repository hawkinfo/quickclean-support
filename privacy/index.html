QuickClean Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026. This policy describes QuickClean for macOS, listed on the App Store as “QuickClean: Storage Cleaner” and developed by zhifei Tao.

Summary

QuickClean analyzes files locally on your Mac. The developer does not automatically collect or upload your files, scan results or usage data. There is no QuickClean account, analytics or advertising. Apple processes purchases through StoreKit.

What QuickClean reads

  • File metadata in the folders you allow it to read: names, sizes, dates and file identities. The App Store build reads only the folders you grant in the open panel. The direct build reads your home folder and the system folders where apps keep caches and logs.
  • The Spotlight index, to count content categories and find large files without walking the disk.
  • Application bundles (Info.plist), to show app names, versions and sizes.
  • Volume information reported by macOS: capacity, available and purgeable space.
  • File contents, only locally and only when you ask: the duplicate finder compares file contents to confirm two files are identical, and the similar-images view reads images to compare them. Nothing read this way leaves your Mac.

QuickClean never opens the contents of your keychains, SSH keys or other credential stores; those locations are excluded from scanning.

What QuickClean stores

  • Check-up and action history in a database at ~/Library/Application Support/QuickClean/ (inside the app container for the App Store build). It holds scan summaries and a journal of items moved to the Trash so they can be restored. You can clear scan history in Settings. Action records are stored separately to support putting items back. Removing the app may leave local app data on your Mac.
  • Settings and folder permissions in the app's preferences.

What QuickClean sends

QuickClean does not automatically send file analysis or usage data to the developer. Apple services and actions you initiate are described below.

  • In-app purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. QuickClean receives product and transaction information to determine unlock access, including purchase and revocation status. It does not receive your payment card details or Apple ID email. See Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Feedback is a file you save and send yourself. It contains app and macOS versions, check-up module results, totals per category and per rule, read errors and your recent actions. File paths are shortened by default; you can choose to include them in full. QuickClean does not send it for you.
  • Links in guidance open in your browser or in System Settings only when you click them.

Support correspondence

If you email the support address, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. These are used to respond and investigate your request, not for advertising or sale. Avoid sending passwords or unrelated personal files. You can request deletion of support correspondence by email, subject to any required retention.

Children

QuickClean does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: zifeit@163.com.

QuickClean 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 6 日。本政策适用于 zhifei Tao 开发的 macOS 应用 QuickClean，其 App Store 名称为“QuickClean: Storage Cleaner”。

摘要

QuickClean 在你的 Mac 本机分析文件。开发者不会自动收集或上传你的文件、扫描结果或使用数据。应用没有 QuickClean 账号、统计或广告。购买通过 Apple 的 StoreKit 处理。

QuickClean 读取什么

  • 你允许读取的文件夹里的文件元数据：名称、大小、日期与文件身份。App Store 版只读取你在打开面板里授权的文件夹；直装版读取个人文件夹以及应用存放缓存和日志的系统文件夹。
  • Spotlight 索引，用来统计内容分类和查找大文件，无需遍历磁盘。
  • 应用包（Info.plist），用来显示应用名称、版本与大小。
  • macOS 报告的磁盘信息：容量、可用与可清除空间。
  • 文件内容，仅在本机、仅在你要求时：重复文件功能比较文件内容以确认完全相同，相似图片功能读取图片进行比较。以这种方式读取的内容不会离开你的 Mac。

QuickClean 从不打开钥匙串、SSH 密钥等凭据存储的内容，这些位置在扫描中被排除。

QuickClean 存储什么

  • 体检与操作历史，存放在 ~/Library/Application Support/QuickClean/（App Store 版在应用容器内），包括扫描摘要和移入废纸篓的记录，用于恢复。可以在设置中清除扫描历史；操作记录单独保存，用于放回项目。删除应用后，本机应用数据可能仍然保留。
  • 设置与文件夹授权，存放在应用偏好设置中。

QuickClean 发送什么

QuickClean 不会自动向开发者发送文件分析结果或使用数据。Apple 服务和你主动发起的操作如下。

  • App 内购买由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。QuickClean 接收商品与交易信息以判断解锁权限，包括购买与撤销状态；不会收到你的支付卡信息或 Apple ID 邮箱。参见 Apple 隐私政策。
  • 反馈是你自己保存并发送的文件，包含应用与 macOS 版本、体检模块结果、各分类与各规则的合计、读取错误和最近的操作。路径默认缩短，可以选择完整包含。QuickClean 不会替你发送。
  • 引导中的链接只在你点击时才在浏览器或系统设置中打开。

客服通信

如果你发送邮件至客服邮箱，开发者会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容及你选择发送的附件。这些信息用于回复与排查问题，不用于广告或出售。请勿发送密码或无关个人文件。你可通过邮件申请删除客服通信记录，但依法需保留的信息除外。

儿童

QuickClean 不会有意收集任何人的信息，包括儿童。

变更

政策如有变化，新版本会以新的生效日期发布在同一地址。
